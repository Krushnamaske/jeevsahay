JivSahayy: AI-Enabled Wildlife Rescue and Animal Help Network

JivSahayy is an AI-enabled wildlife rescue and animal help platform designed to connect people with nearby rescuers, NGOs, veterinarians, and animal service providers. It provides a centralized solution to quickly access help during wildlife sightings, injured animal cases, and emergency situations. The platform aims to bridge the gap between the public and animal welfare services by offering location-based search features, making it easier to find the right help at the right time. It also supports farmers by connecting them with beekeepers for pollination services and helps pet owners find responsible mating services. With the integration of AI technology, users can identify animals by uploading images. JivSahayy strives to create a safer, more responsive, and compassionate environment for both humans and animals.
